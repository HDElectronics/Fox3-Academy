# CoachBox

The instructor's line: what to do now, and why it works.

`text` is the instruction, `why` the reason under it. `tone` colours the lamp — ok for a good state,
caution for a developing threat, warning for an immediate one, dim for a debrief. Keep the copy
pilot-to-pilot: short, concrete, correct DCS vocabulary.

```tsx
<CoachBox tone="caution" text="He is inside your Rne. Notch or drag before he launches."
  why="Inside no-escape range he can trade your turn for closure." />
```
