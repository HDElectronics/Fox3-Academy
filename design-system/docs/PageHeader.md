# PageHeader

A page title with a lede, a placard meta line and right-aligned actions.

`meta` is the small placard line under the title — the jet, radar and module, e.g. 'Su-27 · N001 · FC3'.
`compact` is the variant lab pages use above the viewport.

```tsx
<PageHeader title="Track while scan" lede="Build the picture, then designate." meta="Su-27 · N001 · FC3" />
```
